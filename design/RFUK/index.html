    • Families Belong Together

    2025       Editorial design

    A 3-year research project by Reunite Families UK exploring the impacts of the UK’s spouse/partner migration rules, which are some of the harshest in the world. I designed the book presenting the research and a set of briefing papers. The research was presented at the UK Parliament in 2025. Illustrations and analog photography were an integral part to project's aesthetics, which was tailored to convey a sense of warmth and familiarity. The report (read here) is comprised of essays, case studies and data visualizations.
